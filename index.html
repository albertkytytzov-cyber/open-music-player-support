Built around the library you already own.

Bring your own audio files. Open Music Player keeps the core experience focused on local playback while giving you the controls expected from a full music library.

Control

Choose what gets added

Import individual audio files or selected folders, then refresh a connected folder when its contents change.

Library

Organize without a catalog

Browse songs, albums and artists, build playlists, and use smart collections based on your own listening.

Playback

Keep listening anywhere

Manage the queue, shuffle and repeat, use the equalizer or sleep timer, and control playback from the Lock Screen.

Identification

Recognize a song

Identify nearby music through the microphone or analyze a selected audio file. Identification requires an internet connection.

Nine interface languages.

English, Russian, Bulgarian, Spanish, German, Italian, French, Japanese and Simplified Chinese. The app follows a supported device language automatically, with manual selection available in Settings.
